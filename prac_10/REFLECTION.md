## Estimates

Regarding the **estimates** that you did for practical tasks...

### How was your estimate accuracy usually?

My estimates were mostly accurate, although usually I had overestimated the time I would need to work on these, but only
be around 5 minutes for the larger tasks. For the largest task (project management) I was quite accurate, overestimating
my time of 2 hours and 45 minutes by 15 minutes. This did not, however, include time I spent on later days reviewing my
code.

### How did your estimate accuracy improve or change during the course of the subject?

My estimate accuracy improved over the course of the subject, as I learnt I was a bit faster at coding than I thought.

### What did you learn from doing these estimates?

These estimates helped me to gauge how long I needed to spend not only on this subject, but also other subjects, and
helped me to manage my time better. They also encouraged me to focus while doing the task, as it turned into a 'race
against the clock' scenario. This way I didn't get distracted while I was working.

## Code Reviews

### What have you learned from being reviewed by other people?

Unfortunately I only received 1 review of my code from other people at this point of the subject, which was a bit
devastating. This is not a fault of my own - rather, I was unlucky to be placed in a spot that required me to request
from 4/5 people who weren't participating in the subject. From the 1 review I received, I was reminded of a method I had
forgotten about, which was quite helpful. The fear of people looking at my work also made me want to do my best, and
check over my work more than usual.

### What have you learned from doing code reviews of other people?

I have learnt how to make my code better - by implementing techniques I wasn't using correctly, or avoiding methods that
weren't taught in class. I have also learnt what other people are getting out of this subject, and it made me appreciate
what I have been learning and retaining from this subject.

## Practicals

get as many opportunities to improve and correct my understanding - and other opportunities for students to work
together on tasks. I believe this helps remind students of things they may have forgotten throughout the course.

I was able to implement all knowledge learnt each week into the practicals, and follow all tasks. I read over every
